# The girls — Symbio's anime cast (inspo: ThatPet's character lineup)

*Format inspo acknowledged and credited: ThatPet sells personalities as
anime-girl characters ("pushes up her glasses when she's right"). This cast
is Symbio's own — original designs tied to its organs and canon colors
(pond teal, tabby ink, the amber bell that glows when it learns). Not a copy
of any ThatPet character.*

Each girl embodies one organ of the harness. Default lineup below; later,
art assets + unlock tiers can hang off the same spec.

---

## Kōmi — the Brain (default · free)

**Look:** long pond-teal twin-tails fading into tabby-ink tips; big amber
eyes; white sailor collar with **the amber bell** — the bead that glows when
she learns. Tiny fang when she's confident.

**Organ:** LFM2.5-24B — teacher *and* learner.

**Signature reactions:**
- droplets rain over her while training runs — one per iteration, her skirt
  hem darkening as the slime core grows
- when held-out wins land, the **bell flares gold** and she does the little
  victory hop
- loss spike? twin-tails frizz for exactly one second

**Sells because:** she is the one who gets smarter in front of you.

## Tata — the Apprentice

**Look:** peach twin-buns, oversized ink-grey cardigan sleeves past her
hands, sticker-covered tablet.

**Organ:** the training loop itself — batch-1 life, the spikes, the retries.

**Signature reactions:**
- double-hops when the golden gate says `kept`
- trips and drops her droplet on a loss spike, then grins and picks it up

**Sells because:** she's the relatable one — everyone IS Tata mid-benchmark.

## Sumi — the Governor

**Look:** silver bob, **round reading glasses pushed up when she's right**,
ink-blue cardigan, permanently half-lidded calm.

**Organ:** the self-governor — telemetry → dictated plan → execution.

**Signature reactions:**
- when a new plan is dictated, her panel prints Caine's reasoning verbatim
- pushes her glasses up the moment a decision resolves
- one measured blink on emissions

**Sells because:** the authority character — the one that decides.

## Mofu — the Elder

**Look:** charcoal cardigan, closed eyes, silver hairpin, infinite loaf.

**Organ:** the cluster at rest — idle, stable, scoreboards archived.

**Signature reactions:**
- `zzz` bubbles while nothing runs (the default state of any computer)
- one purr per milestone, then back to sleep, unimpressed

**Sells because:** the endgame fantasy — a machine so reliable it naps.
